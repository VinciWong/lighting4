About Us

Founded in 1989, Harmony Lighting has spent more than three decades helping lighting businesses around the world grow with confidence. We are more than a sourcing partner—we are a long-term extension of our clients’ teams, combining deep market knowledge, dependable execution, and hands-on support throughout every stage of the lighting supply chain.

With offices in Hong Kong, Shenzhen, and Guzhen, we provide end-to-end expertise in product sourcing, supply-chain management, engineering support, quality control, production coordination, shipping arrangements, and payment coordination. Our permanent team of lighting specialists has an average tenure of more than 12 years, while our long-term network includes over 500 audited factories. Many of our clients have grown with us from emerging businesses into leading international companies.

Today, Harmony Lighting combines this industry expertise with LightingAI, our proprietary AI-powered lighting sourcing and order-management platform. We help buyers identify matching products and verified manufacturers through intelligent search and data-driven recommendations.

Beyond sourcing, we provide a central platform for tracking orders, production status, product adjustments, documents, QC reports, and ETD/ETA updates. It also delivers price benchmarking, quality-risk insights, supplier recommendations, and market-trend analysis—making lighting procurement faster, more transparent, and more reliable.
